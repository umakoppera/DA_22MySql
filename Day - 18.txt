TCL Commands:
----------------
TCL stands for transaction control language
Commands:
-------------
commit,rollback,savepoint

transcation:
----------------
it means group of sql operations the group of operations represent
a single unit.

transaction states:
-----------------------
Acid properties:
--------------------
Every transaction to follow the acid properties.

A-Atomicity
C-Consistency
I-Isolation
D-Durability

Log:
---------
Log is nothing but a record.
the transaction should follow the different logs.

undo log:
-------------
it stores the old value
purpose of rollback to change

redo log:
-------------
it is used to read operation

binary log:
-------------
it stores the history of transaction
